APPROVED STORY AND THEMES
The Godlings are the children of the god of the stars and a soldier who died while
they were infants. Unprepared for mortality, their father became overwhelmed by
grief, anger, and despair, abandoned his children, and could no longer uphold the
celestial balance. Their grandmother, the goddess of the earth, secretly raised
them in a forest hut, shielding them from their divine lineage and the intrigues
and revolts among the other gods. Their father has long regretted abandoning them.
One of eight moon goddesses visits the children and seeks their help in bringing
him back to his divine duties. She is NOT an emissary of the other seven: those
seven are involved in the intrigues surrounding the celestial imbalance.
Divine power comes from belief. Initially no creatures believe in the children,
so they have no divine power. Their experiences lead to acts that kindle initial
faith among the world's creatures. That faith, the creatures' perceptions, and
subsequent experiences shape their emerging divinity in a continuing cycle.
Their acts are not necessarily explicit moral choices. Good and evil are not
absolute categories here; consequences and perceptions shape what follows.
The game's objective is to guide the children toward the beings of light they
were destined to be and away from darker impulses, restoring celestial balance.
Do not add lore or implement a morality system without Dominik's direction.
The current demo implements combat only; faith and story progression are not built.

CURRENT GAMEPLAY
Continuous real-time action on discrete grid cells; actors slide smoothly between
centers. Eight movement directions, cardinal attacks only. Walls prevent diagonal
corner cutting. Enemy actors are gridlocked too. Angled perspective, not top-down;
the camera scrolls with Fi. Occupancy reserves origin and target during movement.
Keyboard: WASD and numeric keypad move; arrows attack. Movement and attack direction
are independent. Holding movement repeats after a short delay. Mouse aiming and
left-click attack were explicitly rejected. Controller uses left-stick movement,
right-stick cardinal aiming and trigger attack. Snap to nearest cardinal; exact
stick ties retain prior matching direction. See DemoInput for precise bindings.
Controls are rebindable. F1 opens controls; F2 pauses for annotations.

Fi: 10 health, sword 1.5 damage, three tiles straight ahead. Sword's 0.12-second active
hurtbox follows Fi's actual position during slides, hits each target once per swing,
and respects walls. Attack cooldown 0.4 seconds. After taking damage, Fi is invincible
for 10 gameplay update frames; paused frames do not consume invincibility.
Current tuning defaults: Fi step 0.10 seconds, hold delay 0.18 seconds. Earlier
0.15/0.35 values were superseded during playtesting and remain adjustable.
Regular enemies: 3 health; 2 contact damage, at the attack cooldown cadence;
0.45 seconds per step; sensing/pursuit radius 4 tiles (Euclidean). Outside that
radius they stop pursuing. Show health. Surviving hits knock them one legal tile
backwards over 0.5 seconds, interrupting their current step; blocked recoil staggers
in place. No contact damage during recoil. Boss recoil was not added.
Boss: 50 health, about twice normal enemy visual height. Cardinal proximity within
two tiles triggers a Fi-facing 6-wide by 2-deep swipe: fixed red footprint, warning
2 seconds, damage 4, cooldown 5 seconds between warning starts. Diagonal proximity
alone does not trigger it. Footprint stays where warned so Fi can evade.
Death and boss victory offer Restart.

LEVEL AND METEORS — LATEST REQUIREMENTS
Design/Data/level-sketch.json is the authoritative layout, copied by the builder to
Assets/Godlings/Data/level-sketch.json. 79 columns by 6 rows, 444 floor cells.
Fi starts (1,2). Four enemies start (7,2), (9,4), (16,4), (17,3). Boss (78,2).
Original sketch corridor is retained; a requested 50x6 meteor run occupies columns
20..69 before the boss area 70..78. Coordinates start at lower left.
Meteors activate as Fi approaches the second group, at column 12. Targets begin at
that group's starting line, column 16, and extend through the run and boss area.
Choose visible legal footprints around Fi; do not spawn hazards offscreen as fallback.
First warning is immediate on activation; subsequent warning intervals default to random 2..2.43s (latest screenshot).
The motion panel still permits adjustment up to 10 seconds.
Each drop randomly chooses size 1x1, 2x2, or 3x3. Artwork, shadow, impact hitbox, and
crater obstruction use the SAME size and position. Even sizes center between tiles.
A growing rock-shaped shadow replaces red meteor warnings for 1.5 seconds, then the
impact deals 3 damage to Fi, regardless of size. Crater blocks Fi/enemies for 2 seconds;
enemies route around it. Do not draw a new crater or rock.
Meteors cannot land on live enemies or the boss: selection excludes enemy origin and
reserved step target, and boss tile. If an enemy enters a warned footprint before
impact, CANCEL that drop, rather than teleporting the warned hitbox. No safe visible
location means skip the drop. Meteor damage does not apply to enemies or boss.
Smaller drops sound quieter, higher, and have shorter impact tails.
The boss's logical occupancy is currently one tile despite its larger visual sprite;
ask before changing its gameplay footprint.

TEMPORARY REVIEW TOOLS AND AUDIO
Motion panel pauses play, scrolls, and exposes Fi/enemy step duration, hold delay,
attack cooldown, sensing radius, meteor minimum/maximum interval (2..10), and Fi/enemy
horizontal/vertical artwork offsets. Saved defaults: Fi offset (-0.15,-0.05), enemy offset (0.04,-0.17).
Offsets move visual children only, never grid
positions or hitboxes. Values are session-only rule clones; Restart resets them.
Close and try resumes. Disable EnableMotionPanel/remove this tool for production.
WebReview/index.html adds pause, annotate, and export-notes tools around Unity WebGL.
Review notes use browser localStorage key godlings-review-notes-v1; export JSON to
preserve them. No backend is implemented.
Audio is temporary procedural synthesis: Fi has a young fox yelp (NOT a spoken child
'ouch'); enemies burp; boss hit burp is louder, deeper, about 1.4 seconds, with a
separate non-stacking source. Meteors have falling and impact sounds.
A real fox recording was previewed, but user explicitly chose to keep the synthetic
yelp for now. Do not add downloaded or generated voice recordings without direction.
